# Race The Coco surface brief

Mode: Operate (set up racers, run the race, read results). Audience: TR coworkers. This is an extension of the Planner's visual world, chosen by the user ("keep the overall feel of the other two pages", Planner family, paper track with green lane rules). No new direction round. All three screens in scope: setup, countdown/race, results podium.

Decisions: default racers are Jerome and The Coco. Sprites belong to the racer slot, never to the typed name. Added racers use a generic runner tinted by a chosen colour. Lane order and speed are fully random.

Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Race The Coco is the Planner's printed paper, now holding a race: ruled lanes on warm paper, sprites as the only colour, no costume of a video game around them.

OWN-WORLD: Inherits the Planner world exactly: paper #fafaf5 and #eee9da, forest green #1d4b34 header and 3px rules, orange #d64000 as the single accent, Archivo Black for display, system sans for body, square 3px radii, tabular numerals.

STORY: A coworker lists the racers, starts the race, watches two distinct characters run along ruled lanes, and sees a podium with a clear winner.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Green header band with back link and title. Below it a racers panel (sprite thumbnail, name, remove) beside a sidebar with a lane preview and one green Start Race button.

FORM: Extension of the Planner world (no roll; user-chosen family).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
